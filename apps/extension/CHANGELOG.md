# AndThen extension: what's new

## 0.2.0 (not yet in the stores)

Your routines, in step with your other devices.

- A welcome screen with two ways in: sign in if you already use AndThen, or pick a starter routine.
- Sign in with the 6-digit code from your email. You can close the popup to read the email and come back to the code step.
- Once signed in, the popup catches up every time you open it, and your check-ins go to your other devices a few seconds later.
- The footer shows whether you're synced. From there you can sync now or turn sync off.
- In your account's device list, the extension shows up as "AndThen extension in" your browser.

## 0.1.0 (not yet in the stores)

The first build, for trying out from a folder.

- Click the AndThen icon to see today's routines, with the next habit up front.
- Answer with Done, Did 2 min or Skip, and undo a tap for a few seconds afterwards.
- A ring shows how much of today is done, as in the app.
- With no routines yet, pick a starter routine to begin.
- "Open AndThen" opens the full app in a tab.
