# AndThen extension: what's new

## 0.1.0 (not yet in the stores)

The first build, for trying out from a folder.

- Click the AndThen icon to see today's routines, with the next habit up front.
- Answer with Done, Did 2 min or Skip, and undo a tap for a few seconds afterwards.
- A ring shows how much of today is done, as in the app.
- With no routines yet, pick a starter routine to begin.
- "Open AndThen" opens the full app in a tab.
